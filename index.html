Information the app handles

Location permission is requested while using the app. Granting permission does not itself start collection. Collection begins only after a rider selects a scheduled service, confirms they are onboard, and starts optional trip sharing. During sharing, the app sends GPS coordinates, accuracy and observation time to the tracking backend. On Android, sharing may continue in the background while the foreground-service notification is active.

Accelerometer readings are processed on-device to help infer a possible stop after sustained low movement. Raw accelerometer readings are not uploaded. Saved timetable services are stored on the device. The app does not require an account and does not send a name or advertising identifier. A random temporary trip token is used to authorize and group contribution requests. Backend or network providers may process technical request metadata, such as IP address, under their own privacy terms.

Purposes and sharing

Temporary GPS samples are used to calculate an approximate crowd position for the selected scheduled service. Possible-stop reports contain approximate coordinates, dwell duration, GPS accuracy and observation time. Other users may see aggregated live positions and possible-stop locations for a service. These are crowdsourced estimates, not verified vehicle locations, official stops or arrival predictions.

The backend is hosted by Supabase. Location data is not sold or used for advertising. No account profile is created. Anonymous contributions cannot be verified as belonging to a particular passenger.

Retention

  • Raw GPS samples are deleted after five minutes.
  • A service-level live position expires after 90 seconds without fresh samples.
  • Nearby possible-stop reports are combined into schedule-level aggregates. Aggregates expire 30 days after the latest matching report and do not retain a trip token.
  • Stopping a contribution prevents further reports for that trip. Already uploaded raw samples expire under the five-minute limit.

Choices and controls

Trip sharing is optional. Riders can decline, stop sharing from the tracking screen, or revoke location access in device settings. The app does not have accounts or an account-deletion workflow. Saved services can be removed from the app.

Security

The app communicates with the backend over HTTPS. Database access is mediated by a server-side API and raw location data has a short retention period. Internet transmission and crowd estimates carry residual privacy and accuracy risks; users should not contribute unless comfortable sharing trip location.
